What Is Wheeling

Power Moves Across the Grid.
You Pay the Producer.


Energy wheeling is the transmission of electricity generated by a private, renewable energy producer across the existing national grid infrastructure — delivering it to your facility as if it were generated next door.

The Independent Power Producer (IPP) generates solar or wind energy at their licensed plant. That power is injected into the grid. An equivalent amount is extracted at your meter point. The grid is simply the highway. The agreement — and the price — is directly between you and the producer.

Wheeling does not require you to build anything at your site. No panels, no batteries, no infrastructure changes. Your current electrical connection stays exactly as it is. You simply switch supplier — from Eskom or your municipality to a private IPP offering a fixed, below-Eskom tariff rate.

In plain terms: wheeling is a Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) with a private renewable energy producer, delivered via the national grid — at a rate fixed below what you currently pay, for the duration of your contract.

How It Works

The Energy Path from Producer to You.

Five parties are involved in a wheeling transaction. Understanding each one's role removes the complexity.

IPP / Producer

Generates renewable energy at their licensed solar or wind plant

→

National Grid

Transports power via Eskom's transmission network — the highway

→

Municipality / Distributor

Delivers wheeled energy via the local distribution network to your meter

→

Your Facility

Receives clean, cheaper power at your existing meter — nothing changes on-site

Who Qualifies

Built for High-Consumption Businesses.

Wheeling is a large-scale instrument suited to commercial and industrial operations whose monthly energy demand justifies the deal size — typically 2MW and above.

Large Manufacturing & Processing

Food and beverage, automotive, chemical plants — operations running continuous production lines with consistent, high-volume loads.

Cold Chain & Logistics

Refrigerated warehousing, distribution centres, and logistics hubs running 24/7 — where energy is a major and non-negotiable operational cost.

Data Centres & Tech Infrastructure

Facilities with guaranteed uptime requirements and substantial baseload demand where green credentials support ESG and Scope 2 reporting.

Commercial Real Estate

Large commercial properties and shopping centres supplying multiple tenants — where wheeling reduces bulk supply costs and differentiates the offering.

Mining & Extraction

Surface and underground operations with high, predictable consumption profiles where energy costs represent a significant share of operating expenditure.

Hospitals & Critical Facilities

Healthcare, education campuses, and high-consumption critical facilities that need supply reliability alongside cost reduction and ESG reporting requirements.

2MW+

The entry threshold for wheeling is typically 2MW of contracted capacity. This reflects the deal structures available through licensed IPPs on the Eskom grid. Businesses below this demand level are better suited to onsite solar PV and BESS — which Aethel Energy also originates.

Understanding the Costs

The Charges Involved in Wheeling.

Wheeling involves more than just the tariff you pay the IPP. Third-party charges apply for using the grid — these are factored into every savings model from the outset.

Passed through to client

Wheeling Charge

A fee charged by Eskom Transmission for using the national grid to transport the energy from the IPP's plant to your region. Typically expressed as c/kWh.

Passed through to client

Distribution / Network Charge

A charge levied by the local distributor (Eskom Distribution or municipality) for delivering the wheeled power along the local network to your meter. Varies by region.

Passed through to client

System Losses

A small percentage of energy is lost in transmission. The IPP generates slightly more than you consume to cover grid losses — accounted for in the contracted volume.

Typically absorbed by IPP

Imbalance / Settlement

Where actual generation deviates from contracted delivery, imbalance costs may apply. Most wheeling PPAs allocate this risk to the producer. Confirm in PPA terms.

The Aethel Advantage

Why Wheeling Makes Sense.

For qualifying C&I businesses, wheeling is one of the most cost-effective and structurally straightforward paths to cheaper, cleaner energy in South Africa.

Below-Eskom Tariff, Fixed for the Term

Your PPA rate is fixed below the Eskom tariff and escalates at a pre-agreed rate — typically CPI or a fixed annual percentage — insulating you from Eskom's historic double-digit tariff increases.

No Capital Expenditure Required

A wheeling PPA carries zero upfront cost. No infrastructure build, no procurement, no installation. You sign a PPA and switch supplier — the IPP has already built and financed their plant.

Verified Green Credentials

Your wheeled energy qualifies for renewable energy certificates (I-RECs), enabling verifiable Scope 2 emissions reporting under GHG Protocol standards — supporting your ESG commitments.

No On-Site Infrastructure

Your existing electrical installation remains unchanged. No rooftop assessment, no structural work, no tenant disruption — wheeling is entirely grid-side. Your site is the offtake point, not the generation point.

Scalable to Your Demand

Wheeling can be structured to supply a portion or the majority of your total consumption — from 2MW up to 100MW+. The deal is sized to your actual load profile, not a one-size-fits-all package.

Energy Cost Predictability

A fixed PPA tariff with known escalation gives your finance team multi-year energy cost visibility — removing Eskom tariff uncertainty from your operational budget and enabling better capital planning.
